# Hold–Find–Plan
## Clinician-directed activity kit

**One line:** Picture cubes, a cluttered scene, a required cover, and a language wrap — so a session has a hold step before the search.

Not a medical device. Not a treatment. Not FDA-cleared.

---

### Who it is for
Speech-language pathologists and occupational therapists who want a tabletop activity with a written protocol. Kid track and adult track use the same rules; scenes change.

### What a trial is
1. Client names *n* picture cubes (usually 2–4).
2. Cubes are covered. Wait the assigned delay. No peeking.
3. Find those items in a cluttered scene.
4. Do only the assigned wrap (find only, reverse, sentence, two-step direction, or which-first).
5. Log the trial. Home stays frozen at the last clinic level.

Clinic changes **one** parameter at a time. Home does not self-advance.
