# Hold–Find–Plan planning P&L

Planning model only. Not audited. Assumes a small catalog of clinician materials sold with no disease claims.

## Business model

- Entity: materials company (e.g. MKR Unlimited LLC), not a clinic and not a device firm.
- Customer: SLPs / OTs buying tools the way they buy Super Duper decks.
- Channels year 1: direct / Etsy / Teachers Pay Teachers. Not Onword caseload.
- SKUs: Clinic kit $79, Home sleeve $29 (requires a filled assignment card), Scene pack $19.
- Companion webpage is free with a kit. No subscription in year 1.
- Home kit does not self-advance.

## Down case (half the units, same fixed costs)

Year 1 revenue $4,025. Operating income about **−$390**.

## Cash to start a real first run

- 40 clinic + 20 home + 20 packs inventory ≈ $1,140
- Art + LLC/legal polish + product liability insurance ≈ $1,650
- Working cash buffer $1,000
- **About $3,800** before the first saleable box, not including your time

## What this is not

- Not a $1M year-1 plan
- Not a medical-device raise
- Not a reason to collect data at the treating clinic
- Salary appears only if volume is several times the year-3 line
